💧 Ch2.1 Water Cycle

Key Concepts (重點筆記)

1. Physical States of Water 水的物理狀態

Water exists in three states:

  • Solid (固體): Ice
  • Liquid (液體): Water
  • Gas (氣體): Water vapour or Steam

2. Changes in States 狀態改變

  • Melting 熔化: Solid → Liquid. Absorbs energy. (Melting point is 0 °C)
  • Freezing 凝固: Liquid → Solid. Releases energy. (Freezing point is 0 °C)
  • Boiling 沸騰: Liquid → Gas. Happens at a fixed boiling point (100 °C). Absorbs energy.
  • Evaporation 蒸發: Liquid → Gas. Happens at any temperature. Occurs only on surface. Absorbs energy.
  • Condensation 凝結: Gas → Liquid. Releases energy.

3. The Water Cycle 水循環

  1. Evaporation: Sun's energy turns water into water vapour.
  2. Warm air carries water vapour upwards.
  3. Condensation: Colder upper air cools the vapour, forming water droplets (clouds).
  4. Droplets grow heavy and fall as rain or snow (Precipitation 降水).

4. Rate of Evaporation 蒸發速率

Evaporation increases when:

  • Temperature is higher.
  • Humidity 濕度 is lower.
  • There is more airflow.
  • Surface area exposed is larger.
