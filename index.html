💧 Ch2.1 Water Cycle

Key Concepts (重點筆記)

1. Physical States of Water 水的物理狀態

Water exists in three states:

  • Solid (固體): Ice (冰)
  • Liquid (液體): Water (水)
  • Gas (氣體): Water vapour (水汽) or Steam (水蒸氣)

2. Changes in States 狀態改變

  • Melting 熔化: Solid → Liquid. Absorbs energy 吸收能量. Melting point of ice is 0 °C.
  • Freezing 凝固: Liquid → Solid. Releases energy 釋出能量. Freezing point of water is 0 °C.
  • Boiling 沸騰: Liquid → Gas. Takes place at a fixed boiling point 沸點 (100 °C). Absorbs energy.
  • Evaporation 蒸發: Liquid → Gas. Takes place at any temperature. Occurs only on the surface. Absorbs energy (e.g. sweating keeps us cool).
  • Condensation 凝結: Gas → Liquid. Releases energy. E.g. water vapour turns into water droplets when touching a cold surface.

Note: Steam is invisible 看不見的. The 'white mist' above boiling water is actually tiny condensed water droplets.

3. The Water Cycle 水循環

Water on Earth keeps changing states. The total amount of water is kept constant.

  1. Evaporation: Energy from the Sun powers the cycle. Water evaporates to water vapour.
  2. Warm air carries water vapour upwards.
  3. Condensation: Colder upper air cools the vapour. It condenses into water droplets, forming clouds.
  4. Water droplets grow heavy and fall as rain, snow, or hail 冰雹.

4. Rate of Evaporation 蒸發速率

The rate of evaporation increases when:

  • The temperature is higher.
  • The humidity 濕度 is lower.
  • There is more airflow.
  • The surface area exposed is larger.
